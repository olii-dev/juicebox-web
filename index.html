Everything in the box

A frontend that does the work for you.

🎯

Identification by signature

Juicebox reads each file's header, never its name. A probe order built from real cross-detection risks means the right console is picked every time — extensions don't matter.

🎮

Plays in-app

Bundled libretro cores run every system Juicebox lists, on a dedicated real-time thread. Save states, rewind, cheats and shaders are all built in. No external emulator, ever.

🖼️

Box art from IGDB

Each game is decorated with cover art and metadata from IGDB, scoped by platform. A weak match is left explicitly unmatched rather than attaching the wrong artwork.

✨

Deliberately glossy

A Frutiger Aero interface: the window sits on a sky, covers stand on reflective glass with wet-floor reflections, and every surface carries a hand-drawn gloss sweep.

⏪

Rewind & save states

Hold a key to scrub backwards through recent history. Six save-state slots with thumbnails, plus quick-resume that drops you back where you left off — no cold boot.

🎛️

Full controller support

Any MFi controller is detected automatically. Keyboard and gamepad are both fully remappable per system, and changes apply to a running game without a restart.

CRT

Ten screen shaders

From pristine pixels to a full curved CRT with aperture grille and phosphor bloom — swap shaders live while you play, and remember a favourite per game.

💾

Cheats, your way

Paste a Game Genie or Game Shark code, or import a .cht file. The core decodes its console's format; Juicebox just toggles them on and off, live.

How it works

Drop a folder. That's the setup.

1

Drop your ROMs

Drag in files or a folder. Juicebox hashes each one and works out which console it belongs to — by what's inside the file, not the extension.

2

Art lands automatically

Cover art, summary, publisher and release year arrive from IGDB. Games without a match get a drawn cartridge — never a broken-looking tile.

3

Press play

The right core loads, your battery save restores, and the game runs full-window with shaders, cheats and rewind ready if you want them.

Questions

Before you ask.

Do I need BIOS files or firmware?

No. Juicebox ships no BIOS and needs none — melonDS falls back to its own FreeBIOS, and PCSX-ReARMed ships an HLE BIOS. If you own dumps from your own console, you can drop them in for higher accuracy, but it's entirely optional.

Does Juicebox ship ROMs?

No, and it never will. Juicebox indexes ROMs where you already keep them and never moves or copies them unless you ask it to. It's a frontend, not a source of games.

What about N64 and 3DS?

Both are recognised — drop one in and Juicebox tells you what it is — but not yet playable. N64 cores need a Vulkan context Juicebox can't currently provide, and no 3DS core is published for macOS arm64. The header parsing is written and tested, so it's a matter of solving the emulation, not the formats.

Is it free?

Yes. Juicebox is GPL-3.0 because it bundles libretro cores that are — the same route OpenEmu and RetroArch took. It'll always be free.

What Mac do I need?

macOS 26 (Tahoe) or later, on Apple Silicon. The cores are arm64 and several use dynamic recompilers that want the JIT entitlement.
